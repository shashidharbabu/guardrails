Key takeaways

2. Dense-only retrieval was not enough for this regulatory corpus.
3. Sparse retrieval remained important because of exact legal and compliance wording.
4. Hybrid retrieval without HyDE performed best.
5. RAGAS confirms strong retrieval coverage and strong grounding.
6. The biggest remaining improvement area is answer relevance/directness, not context retrieval.
